About me

I’m a Computer Engineering student at the University of Toronto. I first explored healthcare through my research at WHEEL Lab and by building a model to classify brain MRI scans. At Toronto Hydro, I got to see a different side of engineering through the people and systems that keep the electricity grid running.

Right now, my Engineers Without Borders team and I are developing a predictive model for an atmospheric water generator in Ecuador. One question we’re working through is how to train a useful model with limited data from the site. I love building things and seeing how they work in practice, and I’m curious about where new technology can take us.
